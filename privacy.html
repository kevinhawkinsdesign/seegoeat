Privacy

Your privacy, respected.

Last updated: May 2026

What this site is

SeeGoEat is a personal travel guide curated by Kevin Hawkins. It is a static website — there is no backend, no database, and no user accounts. No personal information is required to browse the site.

Data we collect

We collect no personal data from visitors. Specifically:

  • No sign-up, login, or account is required.
  • No cookies are set by this site.
  • No analytics scripts (e.g. Google Analytics) are loaded.
  • No form submissions are stored — the "Suggest a place" form sends an email directly to Kevin and is not logged anywhere.

AI Search

The "✦ Ask AI" search feature sends your typed query to the Anthropic API to generate recommendations. When you use AI search:

  • Your search query and the site's place index are sent to Anthropic's servers to generate results.
  • Anthropic may log API requests per their own privacy policy.
  • No query history is stored by SeeGoEat itself.

If you do not wish to use this feature, simply use the standard text search instead — it runs entirely in your browser with no external requests.

Third-party services

This site loads the following third-party resources:

  • Google Fonts — typefaces (Playfair Display, DM Sans) are loaded from Google's CDN. Google may log font requests per their privacy policy.
  • Anthropic API — used only when you click "✦ Ask AI", as described above.

No advertising networks, tracking pixels, or social media embeds are used.

Local storage

This site may use your browser's localStorage to remember your last-viewed city or UI preferences. No personally identifiable information is stored there. You can clear it at any time via your browser settings.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Use the "Suggest a place" form on the main site, or reach Kevin directly through kevinthecreator.com.
