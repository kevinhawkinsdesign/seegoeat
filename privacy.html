Privacy

Your privacy, respected.

Last updated: July 2026

What SeeGoEat is

SeeGoEat is a personal travel guide curated by Kevin Hawkins. It comes in two forms:

  • This static website — no backend, no accounts, browse freely.
  • The SeeGoEat mobile app (iOS & Android) — adds optional sign-in so you can log visits, write personal notes, and rate places.

Sign-in is optional. You can browse the full catalog of cities and places without an account.

Data the website collects

The website collects no personal data:

  • No sign-up, login, or account is required.
  • No cookies are set.
  • There is no contact form or submission mechanism.

Data the mobile app collects

The app collects the minimum necessary to power its features:

  • Email address — only if you sign in. Used as your account identifier and to send a one-time sign-in code. We do not send marketing email.
  • Apple ID identifier — only if you choose "Sign in with Apple". Apple may share a relay email which we use the same way as a regular email.
  • Your visits, notes, and ratings — saved to your account so you can sync across devices. Only you can see, edit, share, or delete them.
  • Photos you attach — to a logged visit, or as your profile picture. Only you can see or delete visit photos; a profile picture is visible to anyone if you've set a public handle.
  • Profile info — handle, display name, and bio, only if you choose to set them. These are public if you set a handle; aggregate visit counts are shown, never your notes.
  • Lists — collections of places you create. Private by default.
  • Precise location — only when you tap "Near me" on the Map tab. Read on-demand from your device to show nearby places, used entirely on your device, and never sent to or stored on our servers.
  • Bookmarks — stored in your account if signed in, otherwise locally on your device only.
  • Search queries — the text you search with is sent to our search function to find matching places (see "Search" below). Not linked to your identity, not logged.
  • Anonymous usage analytics — see "Analytics" below.

You can delete your account and all associated data — visits, photos, lists, bookmarks, and profile — at any time from the Profile tab.

Search

Both the website and the app use semantic search so a query like "romantic rooftop" can match places that don't use those exact words. Your search text is sent to a server-side function which asks Voyage AI to generate a numeric representation of its meaning, then matches it against our place catalog. Voyage AI receives only the query text — no account information, device ID, or other identifying data. Queries are not logged or stored by us. Voyage AI's privacy policy: voyageai.com/privacy.

Analytics

The mobile app uses PostHog to understand which screens and features are used, and to diagnose crashes. PostHog receives:

  • A randomly-generated anonymous device identifier (not tied to your name).
  • App version, OS, device model, and approximate region inferred from IP.
  • Screen views and high-level event names (e.g. "place opened", "visit logged"). The contents of your notes are never sent.

PostHog's privacy policy: posthog.com/privacy. No advertising networks, tracking pixels, or social-media SDKs are used.

Where data is stored

Account data (email, visits, notes, ratings, photos, lists, profile info) is stored with Supabase, which provides our authentication, database, storage, and search functions. Row-level security ensures only you can access your own private data. Supabase's privacy policy: supabase.com/privacy.

City photos are fetched from the public Wikipedia REST API. Photos you upload (visit photos, profile pictures) are stored in Supabase Storage.

Delete your account

You can delete your account and all associated data — visits, photos, notes, ratings, lists, bookmarks, and profile — at any time from Profile → Delete account inside the app. This is immediate and irreversible.

If you no longer have the app installed, email kevin@seegoeat.com from the address associated with your account and we'll delete it within 7 days.

Your rights

You can at any time:

  • Sign out and continue browsing anonymously.
  • Delete any individual visit, note, or rating from the place screen.
  • Delete your entire account and all stored data from the Profile tab (irreversible).
  • Disable analytics on the device level via your OS privacy settings.

Children

SeeGoEat is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
